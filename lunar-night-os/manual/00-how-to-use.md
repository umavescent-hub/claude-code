# How to use Kubrick

Kubrick is one page that runs on the Pixel. It carries a night from the walk-in to the upload: the camera settings, the shots, the CapCut cut, the look, the type, the assets and the hook. This chapter covers how the page is laid out, how files reach your phone, and how to study the material so it sticks.

## The pipeline: nine modules in order

The rail along the top numbers the modules in the order the work happens. Each stage hands the next one something real.

| Module | What it does for you |
|---|---|
| **01 Rig** | Pre-flight for the Pixel 10 Pro XL: the camera app, the dials and the checks, set the same way every night. |
| **02 Shot Bible** | What to shoot, plus the ticket rail every module shares. Queue a shot, fire it, put it on the pass or 86 it. |
| **03 The Cut** | The CapCut Mobile edit: cut rhythm, speed ramps, the audio spine, auto-cut, captions, an edit plan and music edits cut to the beat. |
| **04 Transitions** | Transitions for the review and documentary cuts, built in CapCut. Lunar Night reels stay on hard cuts. |
| **05 Grade** | The LunarNight v2 looks as real .cube files, plus the CapCut slider recipe. |
| **06 Type & HUD** | Reel text and title cards in the house fonts. |
| **07 Forge** | Fable 5.1 designs titles, plates and sound as code; your phone renders the MP4 and WAV files. |
| **08 Aura** | The psychology of the first three seconds: hooks, titles and covers. |
| **09 Manual** | This reference, the search, and the recall deck. |

Tickets, settings and recall dates are kept in this browser on this phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Saving files in claude.ai

Every save button hands you a real file. Inside claude.ai the page can't write to your phone by itself, so each save works like this:

1. Tap the button once. One tap saves one file.
2. claude.ai asks you to confirm. Confirm it, every time. If you cancel, nothing is saved and the page tells you.
3. The phone stores it like any other download.

Markdown, text, PNG, MP4 and ZIP files arrive as they are. Two kinds don't: **.cube** looks and **.wav** sound arrive wrapped in a **.zip** of the same name, because claude.ai only hands over certain file types.

> [!TIP] Unzip a .cube or .wav
> Open `Files by Google › tap the zip › Extract`. The .cube or .wav lands next to the zip, ready for Blackmagic Camera or CapCut.

> [!VERIFY]
> In Files by Google the zip usually sits under Browse, then Downloads. Labels move between versions, so check once.

If a save says it is unavailable, the page is open in a preview. Open it from claude.ai, or use the offline copy.

## The offline copy

The footer has **Save offline copy (.html)**. It saves all of Kubrick as one HTML file. Open that file in Chrome and Kubrick runs without claude.ai, which helps on a dark lot with no signal.

What changes offline:

- **Fable 5.1 buttons can't reach Claude.** Every Fable panel also shows its prompt as text: copy it into a Fable 5.1 chat instead.
- **Files download straight to the phone**, with no confirm step and no zip.
- **Each copy keeps its own notes.** Tickets, the Brand Brain and recall dates saved in claude.ai don't carry into the offline copy, or back.
- **Web fonts need a connection.** With no signal, title cards draw in the phone's fallback fonts.
- **Video renders** are frame-accurate MP4 when the phone has a connection. With no signal they fall back to recording in real time, which can drop frames.

## Keep the screen awake

Flip **Keep screen awake** in the footer and the screen stays on while this page is open, so a checklist or the study timer doesn't go dark mid-step. It lets go while you're in another app and comes back when you return. If the phone refuses, the page tells you: set Screen timeout to 10 minutes in Android Settings instead.

## The toolchain

Four tools carry the whole pipeline.

| Tool | Its job |
|---|---|
| **Pixel 10 Pro XL** | Camera and edit bay. Blackmagic Camera is the default camera app; Pixel Camera with Pro controls is the fallback. |
| **CapCut Mobile (Android)** | The only editor. It can't load .cube files, so the look is recorded into the clip in Blackmagic Camera or rebuilt with the slider recipe. |
| **Claude** | Runs Kubrick inside claude.ai; the offline copy runs without it. |
| **Fable 5.1** | A Claude model: text and images go in, text comes out. It writes the code, the numbers and the prompts, and the phone renders the files. It never hands you a video or audio file by itself. |

> [!VERIFY] CapCut and .cube files
> Your playbook lists `Adjust › LUT › Import`; the runbook says CapCut Mobile can't load .cube files. Check once on the phone. No LUT import under Adjust means you bake the look in Blackmagic Camera or use the slider recipe in Grade.

## How to study: the 15-minute block

This is the protocol from your playbook. You learn by pulling the answer out, not by reading it again, so each block ends in a quiz.

One module per block, timer on, phone face down except for CapCut:

1. **2 min: read the law lines.** Only the bolded laws of one module. Nothing else.
2. **8 min: drill in CapCut.** Do the module's drill on real footage.
3. **5 min: recall.** Answer that module's cards in the Recall tab without scrolling up. Wrong means read that one line again, nothing else.

The rules that keep the block working:

- Finish the block before you open anything else.
- Stand up, change rooms, or shoot the drill outside.
- **Two modules a day, max.** Sleep is when the grade settles in.
- **Run the deck again on day 2 and day 7.** Spacing beats one long session.
- Under 12 out of 15 (80%) means rerun that block tomorrow.

The Recall tab runs all of it: the block timer with its three phases, the playbook's 15 cards, and the day 2 and day 7 dates counted from your first session. A chapter that ends in recall cards gets a deck of its own, and **Drill these cards** at the end of the chapter opens it.

> [!RECALL] How long is one study block, and how is it split? :: 15 minutes: 2 min on the law lines, 8 min of CapCut drill, 5 min of recall

> [!RECALL] You get a card wrong. What do you do? :: Read that one line again, nothing else

> [!RECALL] How many modules a day, at most? :: Two

> [!RECALL] When do you run the deck again? :: Day 2 and day 7 after the first session

> [!RECALL] A .cube file arrives as a .zip. How do you open it? :: Files by Google, tap the zip, Extract
