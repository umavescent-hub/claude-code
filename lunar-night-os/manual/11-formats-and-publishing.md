# Formats & publishing

Four formats, four sets of rules. Know which one you are making before you press record.

## The four formats

**LAW: the format decides length, sound and how many cuts you may use.**

| Format | Length | Sound | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketplace Review | 25–40 s reach cut, or 65–90 s Rewards cut | Voice 100, music 10–20 | Verdict after 60 s on the Rewards cut, at most 2 transitions |
| Lunar Night Reel | 5–7 s single, or a 5–7 shot montage | Ambient 20–30 or night sound 100, no SFX | Hard cuts only, car pass third or last |
| Road to Three Stars | Varies | Voice spine | Hook 0–3 s, 1.8 s montage, 4.5 s breathe |
| R&D Lab | Varies | Foley up, sizzles +4 dB | 4K24 story, 4K60 for pours and sears |

## The first three seconds

**LAW: something changes every 3 s, and the first 3 s carry a shock, a transient and a title.**

## Publishing

- Export at 1080p or 4K, 9:16 for TikTok and Reels.
- Cover: the money frame with a three-word title inside the safe zones.
- Post, then log one change in The Cut so the next plan learns.

## The 15-minute drill

Two minutes reading the law lines, eight minutes drilling in CapCut, five minutes on recall. Two modules a day at most. Recall again on day 2 and day 7.

## The set

**LAW: cleanliness is godliness.**

Wipe the lens in circles from the centre out. Clear the board. Nothing on the pass you would not serve.
