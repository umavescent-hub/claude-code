# Fable 5.1 & Claude in the loop

Claude plans. You shoot and cut. Then you tell Claude what you changed, and the next plan is better.

## What Fable does here

**LAW: Fable writes plans and words, never your taste.**

- **Edit Plan** (The Cut): a cut sheet and tap-by-tap CapCut steps from your clip list.
- **Caption polish** (The Cut › Captions): clean four-word cards and an eight-word hook.
- **Music Edit** (The Cut › Music Edit): a layer-by-layer multi-layer cut on the beat.
- **Fable Studio**: shot ideas, prompts and assets.

## The Brand Brain

Every Fable prompt carries your style guide, the shots you approved and the changes you logged. The more you log, the closer the first draft lands.

## The learning loop

**LAW: after every post, log one change.**

In The Cut › Edit Plan › "After you post", write what the plan said, what you did instead, and why. That note rides into every future prompt.

## What to paste, what never to paste

Paste clip names, durations, what happens in each, the verdict and the score. Never paste passwords, guest names, private messages or anything from the restaurant's systems.

## The multilayer-music-edit skill

`skills/multilayer-music-edit` lets any Claude chat run the music-cut method with you: beat maths, the layer stack, cut patterns and CapCut steps.

> [!VERIFY] Installing a skill
> In claude.ai open `Settings › Capabilities › Skills › Upload` and add the zipped folder. The menu path may differ on your plan.
