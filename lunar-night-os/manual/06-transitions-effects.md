# Transitions & effects

A transition is a promise that the two shots belong together. Make it in camera first, then finish it in CapCut.

## Format limits

**LAW: format decides how many transitions you get.**

| Format | Allowed |
|---|---|
| Marketplace Review | At most 2, about 0.2 s each |
| Lunar Night Reel | None. Hard cuts only |
| Road to Three Stars | Full set |

## The seven moves

**LAW: shoot the seam, don't fake it.**

1. **Whip-pan seam**: matching flicks at the end of A and the start of B. In CapCut add Motion blur: Blur 65, Blend 40, Direction Both, Times Twice.
2. **Foreground wipe**: a knife, your back or a copper pan crosses the lens. Put clip B on an overlay, `Overlay › Mask › Split`, Feather 18, keyframe the line across 12 frames.
3. **Match-action + sound bridge**: cut on the impact frame (palm on dough → walk-in door), pre-lap the sizzle 15 frames.
4. **Zoom-through portal**: 5x punch into a dark pan. Scale 100% → 280% over 8 frames, ease-in, a chromatic burst and a sharpen flash.
5. **Halation**: duplicate on overlay, Screen or Lighten, Blur 20–40, warm Temp, Opacity 25–45%. Strong and warm for Dreamcore, faint and cool for the dark look.
6. **VHS 4:3 snap**: confessions and failures in a 4:3 pillarbox, snapping to clean full frame on the frame the dish hits the pass.
7. **Punch-in**: 2–3 per video, 8–15%, on the bite, the money shot and the verdict.

> [!VERIFY] Motion blur and Mask names
> In CapCut Mobile check `Effects › Video effects` for Motion blur, and `Overlay › Mask` for Split. Labels vary by version.

> [!RECALL] Transitions allowed in a Night Reel? :: None, hard cuts only
> [!RECALL] Motion blur settings for a whip seam? :: Blur 65, Blend 40, Both, Twice
> [!RECALL] Foreground wipe feather and length? :: Feather 18 over 12 frames
> [!RECALL] Zoom-through scale and length? :: 100% to 280% over 8 frames, ease-in
> [!RECALL] Punch-ins per video? :: 2–3, at 8–15%
> [!RECALL] Halation opacity? :: 25–45%
