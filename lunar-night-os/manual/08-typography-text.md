# Typography & text

Type is a voice. Use one per format and never shout in two.

## The system

**LAW: one display face, one caption face, one accent colour.**

- UI and titles: TikTok Sans (CapCut's own family).
- Reel faces: Oswald or Antonio, condensed.
- Captions: Inter Bold, white, black outline.
- Night accent: lime #D6DB05.

## By format

**LAW: captions are read in under a second or they fail.**

| Format | Captions | Titles |
|---|---|---|
| Marketplace Review | Inter Bold, white, black outline, 3–4 words a line, low but above the bottom quarter | Score and verdict card after 60 s |
| Lunar Night Reel | Lime #D6DB05, condensed lowercase, upper third, first 3 s only | None |
| Road to Three Stars | Workhorse captions | Chapter cards in the reel face |

## Safe zones

Keep text out of the top 130 px, the right 140 px and the bottom 484 px of a 1080×1920 frame: the app's buttons live there.

## Captions in CapCut

`Captions › Auto captions › English › Generate`, then fix every line. Use Fable in The Cut › Captions to clean them to four words or fewer with Tewa, Spanish and dish names spelled your way.

> [!VERIFY] Font import
> In CapCut Mobile check `Text › Font` for an upload or import option. If it is missing, use CapCut Desktop or the Type module's PNG mattes.

## Mattes

The Type module exports transparent PNG and MOV title mattes. Add them with `Overlay › Add overlay`, scale to fit, and keep them off the safe-zone edges.
